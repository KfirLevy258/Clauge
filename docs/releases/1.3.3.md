**BLINK 1.3.3 is about updates that finish.** An update you approved could stop half-way and say nothing at all, leaving the app updated, the board on its old version, and no way to ask again until the board was unplugged. That cannot happen now, and if an update does fail the panel tells you.

## What changed

- **An update that gets interrupted finishes itself.** Installing from the board is two steps -- the app on your computer first, then the board -- and the app restarts in the middle of them. If anything went wrong in that moment, even a network hiccup lasting a second, your approval was thrown away: the app came back, decided there was nothing to do, and the board sat on its old version with nothing on screen to say why. Your approval now survives that moment and the install finishes on its own, usually within seconds.
- **A failed update says so, on the panel.** Before, the board was told "up to date" -- which was not true -- and showed nothing. It now says the update failed and gives the reason, in the same place it told you an update was ready.
- **The board offers the update again.** The offer was shown once per start-up, so a tap that led nowhere left the board unable to ask a second time until it was power-cycled. A failure is not a "Later": the offer comes back.
- **After an update, the board says what it got.** A tick, the new version, and how much changed -- with a **What's new** button that pages through the releases you skipped, if you were more than one behind.
- **"App is old" no longer appears when the app is current.** The board worked this out by comparing your app against its own firmware, which is not the same question, and got it wrong on any board running ahead of the published release. It now reports only what the app itself has found.
- **A tap on "Update now" during an app update no longer goes nowhere.** If the app happened to be restarting at the moment you tapped, the tap was swallowed: nothing installed, nothing said. Reported from a desk on 2026-09-09.
- **The update box cannot push its own OK button off the screen**, and it no longer sits there after the thing it was offering has gone away.
- **Gestures stay where they belong.** A swipe across a message on screen could reach the page behind it and start changing pages, or open settings from the strip down either edge.
- **The log keeps about five weeks instead of a fortnight**, and a repeating fault is now recorded once with a count rather than thousands of times. If you ever send us a log after something went wrong, the part that explains it is far more likely to still be in there.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

If the board is showing an older version after you update the app, give it a minute and it will offer the firmware; that is the half this release is about, and it now picks itself back up.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
